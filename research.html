Research Interests

I work at the boundary between intelligent software and the systems that make it practical, with particular emphasis on:

  • Systems for AI: efficient, reliable runtimes for agentic and generative workloads
  • Heterogeneous Edge AI: correctness-aware placement across CPUs, iGPUs, and NPUs
  • Compiler and Runtime Optimization: reducing compilation, memory, and state-migration costs
  • Program Analysis and Systems Security: turning software fixes into reusable bug detectors

Compiler-Aware Heterogeneous Edge AI
Xuming Huang (advised by Yiying Zhang, WukLab @ UC San Diego)
WukLab Research Internship — Ongoing, Summer 2026
project / evidence / compiler patch / PR branch / OpenVINO NPU Compiler

Building a correctness-first runtime for private edge agents that coordinates CPU/iGPU/NPU placement, growing KV state, and one-ahead NPU specialization. Across 149 reproducible experiment runs, I traced the bottleneck into OpenVINO's NPU compiler, cached 33.33M repeated dependency-vector sorts, and reduced full compile time by 3.34–3.68% in 12/12 production A/B trials. A real 1K→2K future variant compiled in 14.68 s and switched with zero boundary wait.
